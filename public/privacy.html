Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SAVEZO handles information when you use our website and tools.

2. Information you provide

SAVEZO does not require an account for normal use of its public tools. If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and message content.

3. Tool data

Many SAVEZO calculators process the values you enter in your browser to produce a result. We do not need those calculator values to identify you or create an account.

4. Cookies and third-party services

SAVEZO may use essential technologies or third-party services in the future for website operation, security, analytics or advertising. If such services are introduced, this policy may be updated to describe the relevant data practices.

5. Advertising

If advertising is enabled, advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies subject to their own policies and applicable choices available to users.

6. Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through the website, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website or its services change. The latest version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@savezo.in.
